# Contributor guides

These guides are for someone who wants to change the specification corpus, whether by correcting it, extending it, or proposing something new. They assume you are working in this repository and can open its governance and process documents by name.

Knowledge Islands is pre-v1, so no new KIP is registered today. Use these guides to judge where a concern belongs now and to prepare for the process that applies after the v1 boundary review.

## Guides

- [Deciding whether a change needs a KIP](deciding-whether-a-change-needs-a-kip.md) - route a change to an editorial fix, a new version, a superseding specification, or a new proposal, and know what to do with it while the repository is dormant.
- [Raising a KIP](raising-a-kip.md) - prepare a proposal directory, obtain a number, take it through review, and recover from the common problems on the way.
